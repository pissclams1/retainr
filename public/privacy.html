Privacy Policy

Effective June 15, 2026

Local file processing

The current PublishReady validation beta processes selected files locally in your browser. PublishReady does not intentionally upload or store the contents of those files on its servers.

Technical data

The beta may use standard hosting logs generated by GitHub Pages or other infrastructure providers. These logs can include information such as browser type, approximate location derived from an IP address, requested pages, and timestamps. PublishReady does not sell personal information.

Third-party services

The site loads software libraries and fonts from third-party content-delivery networks. Those providers may receive standard request information when your browser loads those resources.

Payments and accounts

Paid exports, account creation, and checkout are not currently enabled. This policy will be updated before those features launch.

Your responsibility

Do not select files you are not authorized to use. You remain responsible for reviewing files before submitting them to Amazon KDP, ACX, or another publishing platform.

Changes

This policy may change as the product develops. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
